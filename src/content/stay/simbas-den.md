Simba's Den is the one log cabin at the camp: two bedrooms, two bathrooms and a living room of warm timber that opens onto a deck over the river.

The deck has a table for six, made for long breakfasts and private dinners as the light goes. Families and small groups have room to spread out, with the camp's pool, bar and fire a short walk away.
