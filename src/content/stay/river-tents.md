The river tents stand in a line along the bank, each with its own terrace looking over the water. Hippos call through the night and the first birds start before dawn.

Inside, canvas and dark timber, a wide bed and a writing desk. The terrace is where most guests spend the quiet hours between drives, with a coffee and a pair of binoculars.
