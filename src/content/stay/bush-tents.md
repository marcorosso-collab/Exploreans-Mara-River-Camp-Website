The bush tents sit a little further into the trees, with nearly twice the room of a river tent and a terrace that looks out into the bush.

There is space here for a family or for friends travelling together, and the same evening ritual as everywhere in camp: the bed turned down, the lamps lit and the sounds of the Mara outside the canvas.
