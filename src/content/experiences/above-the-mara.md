The day starts in the dark. The vehicle leaves camp from 5am for the launch site, about an hour away, and the balloon fills as the sky begins to lighten.

Then you are up, drifting over the grassland, the forest and the river as the herds wake beneath you. Depending on the wind, the flight lasts between 45 minutes and an hour and a quarter.
