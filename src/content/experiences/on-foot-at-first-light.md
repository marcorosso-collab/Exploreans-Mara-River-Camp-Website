On foot, the Mara is a different place. Without an engine you hear the francolins calling, the grass brushing your legs and the river moving below the bank.

Your guide grew up on this land. A print in the mud, a broken branch, the alarm call of a bird: each one is a line in the story of the night, and your guide reads it aloud as you walk.
