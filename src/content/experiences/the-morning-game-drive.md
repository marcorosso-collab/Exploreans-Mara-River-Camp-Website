The Mara shows a different side at each hour. In the morning the light is low and gold and the predators are still moving. In the afternoon the herds drift toward water. A full day goes further, with lunch in the shade of an acacia.

Your guide knows these plains by season and by the hour, and reads the sky, the grass and a single bird to find what you would pass by alone.
