The Maasai have lived alongside the wildlife of the Mara for generations. With a guide from the camp, you are welcomed into a nearby village as a guest: how the homes are built, the place of cattle in daily life, the beadwork passed down through families.

The singing and the adumu, the jumping dance, are part of village life. You are welcome to watch, and often invited to join.
