The Mara shows a different side at each hour. In the morning the light is low and gold and the predators are still moving. In the afternoon the herds drift toward water. A full day goes further, with lunch from the cool box wherever the wildlife is.

Your guide knows these plains by season and by the hour, and reads the sky, the grass and a single bird to find what you would pass by alone. Choose a private jeep for your own party or join a shared drive.
