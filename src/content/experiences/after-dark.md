When the day drives end, the night shift begins. At 8pm the vehicle heads into the Ole Sugut Conservancy, with your guide and an armed ranger, and the spotlight starts its slow sweep across the grass.

For two hours, the Mara you have not yet seen: eyes in the dark, the animals that move only at night, and a sky full of stars above the vehicle.
