The morning starts in the vehicle, out of camp and into the Ole Sugut Conservancy. Then the walking begins: a guided trek along a moderately hilly route, with your guide reading the ground as you go and an armed ranger alongside.

At the top, a scenic lookout and a gourmet packed lunch, with the Mara spread out below. In the afternoon the walk continues to a distinctive rocky outcrop, the place for photographs as the light softens, before the drive back to camp.
