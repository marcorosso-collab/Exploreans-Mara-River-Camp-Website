The afternoon drive ends somewhere with a view: a rise above the plains, a bend of the river. The camp team has been there first, and a table is laid in the grass with cold drinks and something to eat.

Then the best part of the day: the light going gold, then amber, then gone, and the first stars on the drive back to the fire.
