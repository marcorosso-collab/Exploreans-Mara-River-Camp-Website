Some meals belong outside. After the morning drive, breakfast is laid under a tree on the plains: eggs, warm bread, fresh fruit and coffee, with the grassland waking up around you.

On a full-day drive, lunch comes the same way, in the shade of an acacia with the plains stretching out in every direction.
