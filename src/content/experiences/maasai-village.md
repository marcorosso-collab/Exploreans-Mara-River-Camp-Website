The Maasai have lived alongside the wildlife of the Mara for generations. A visit to a neighbouring village is a chance to meet a Maasai family on their own terms: how the homes are built, the place of cattle in daily life, the beadwork passed down through families.

Songs and dances are part of village life, and you are often invited to join.
