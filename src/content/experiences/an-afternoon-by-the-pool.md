Explore within, at the pace of the river. In the middle of the day the animals rest in the shade, and so does the camp.

The pool sits among the trees with sunbeds and shade around it. Swim, read, sleep. A cold drink arrives from the Zebra Bar, and the afternoon drive can wait.
