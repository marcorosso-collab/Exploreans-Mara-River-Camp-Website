Every evening ends in the same place: around the fire on the lawn above the river. The hosts bring a nightcap from the Zebra Bar, someone tells the story of the day's drive and the sky fills with stars above the river.

Listen and the night is full: a hippo grunting in the shallows, a hyena far off, the crickets in the grass.
